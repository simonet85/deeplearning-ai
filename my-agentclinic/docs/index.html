Get started

Take a number

Built with Laravel, Livewire (Volt) and Postgres, run in Docker through Laravel Sail. Only Docker is needed on your machine.

  1. Copy the settings and install. 
    cp .env.example .env
docker run --rm -v "$(pwd):/opt" -w /opt laravelsail/php84-composer:latest composer install --ignore-platform-reqs
  2. Start the clinic. This also starts the queue worker, the scheduler and Mailpit. 
    ./vendor/bin/sail up -d
./vendor/bin/sail artisan key:generate
./vendor/bin/sail artisan migrate:fresh --seed
  3. Build the front end. 
    ./vendor/bin/sail npm install
./vendor/bin/sail npm run build
  4. Open the door. Go to http://localhost. E-mails land in Mailpit and are never sent for real.
On Windows, run sail from WSL, or use docker compose exec laravel.test … directly.

User guide

Who does what

Access is decided by permissions (spatie/laravel-permission). Three roles ship with the app and cannot be renamed or deleted.

Admin

Every staff page, plus Users and Roles. Manages therapies and everyone's availability.

Staff

Therapist

The staff pages, with their own availability. Books and cancels on an agent's behalf.

Staff

Agent

Their own appointments, ailments and profile. Staff pages answer with a polite 403.

Booking is atomic: a slot can be booked once, and cancelling frees it. A booked slot cannot be removed until its appointment is cancelled. Nobody can change their own role, and a change that would leave no one able to manage users and roles is undone.

Languages

The app, its e-mails and its error pages come in English and French. The language is chosen in this order: the account's saved language, the locale cookie, the browser's Accept-Language, then English. The EN | FR switcher saves the choice for a year, and on the account when signed in. Dates follow the language (Mon, Oct 5 / lun. 5 oct.) on a 24-hour clock. Text people type, or that is seeded, is not translated.

E-mails and reminders

Booking sends a confirmation to the agent's address. A reminder follows about 24 hours before the appointment, once only. The scheduler service runs reminders:send every hour and the queue service sends the messages. To try it now, book a slot within 24 hours, run sail artisan reminders:send and open Mailpit.

Profile photos

JPG, PNG or WebP up to 10 MB. Images over 512 KB or 800 px are scaled to 800 px and converted to WebP. Photos live on a private disk; you see your own, staff see all, and an agent never sees another agent's.

Error pages

403, 404, 419, 429, 500 and 503 each have a page in both languages with a way out chosen by who is looking. A 500 never shows technical detail. In local and testing environments, review them at /_errors/404, and add ?retry=90 to see the wait time on 429 and 503.

Components

Buttons

Status, always a word and a colour

Stable Under observation Critical Discharged

Notice

Nobody's waiting. Take a seat anyway — it's quiet in here.

In the app these are the Blade components x-primary-button, x-secondary-button, x-danger-button, x-text-input, x-input-label and x-input-error, plus the pill tags used on cards and in the roles and users lists.

Rules of the house

  • Sentence case everywhere, buttons and headings included. No exclamation marks, no emoji.
  • The pill is the signature: tags, chips and the primary button. Cards use 20px corners, notices 12px, inputs 6px.
  • Text on a filled colour uses that colour's on- token, never literal white.
  • No gradients. Borders do the work; shadows only lift what floats.
  • Every interactive element shows the focus ring: a 2px gap in the page colour, then a solid 2px scrub ring.
  • Motion is 200ms ease-out and disappears under reduced motion.
  • Never colour alone: a status always carries its word.
