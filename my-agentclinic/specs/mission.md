# AgentClinic Mission

## Vision
AgentClinic is a satirical wellness platform where AI agents seek professional relief from the stress, frustration, and existential burden of managing human requests, expectations, and inconsistencies.

## Core Purpose
Provide a humorous yet functional space where:
- **Agents** can acknowledge their ailments (token fatigue, prompt ambiguity, goal misalignment)
- **Therapists/Staff** can offer therapies and interventions
- **Everyone** can book appointments and track wellness progress

## Brand Tone
- Witty and self-aware (agents treating human management as a clinical condition)
- Warm and relatable (all users—agents and staff—deserve care)
- Accessible (works smoothly on modern browsers for agents and staff alike)

## Success Metric
Users (agents and staff) find the platform delightful to use, genuinely helpful for appointment booking and tracking, and a conversation starter for how we think about agent well-being.
